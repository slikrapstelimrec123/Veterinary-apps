# Roadmap

## Current

- reliable owner authentication;
- pet profiles and photos;
- owner-managed medical history and documents;
- medications, feeding, events, and reminders;
- announcements and pet transfers;
- privacy, export, and account deletion;
- TestFlight quality and security hardening.

## Next

- improve offline/error recovery;
- expand owner-side reminders;
- improve document organization;
- accessibility and localization audit;
- release analytics that do not expose medical content.

## Not Planned

Clinic administration, doctor accounts, appointment booking, clinic reviews, clinic subscriptions, payments, chat, AI diagnosis, insurance, and telemedicine.
