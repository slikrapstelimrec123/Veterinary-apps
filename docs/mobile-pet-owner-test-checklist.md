# Mobile Owner Test Checklist

- Home opens with current pet and care information.
- Pet creation validates required fields and saves the photo.
- Pet edits appear immediately on every dependent screen.
- Medical visits are created by the owner without clinic or appointment fields.
- Documents remain private and open only for the owner.
- Medication, feeding, and event create/edit/delete actions persist after restart.
- Announcements load immediately and preserve pet photo and editable phone number.
- Transfers and notifications update immediately.
- Settings include notification preferences, invite friends, legal pages, export, deletion, and logout.
- Empty, loading, offline, and retry states are understandable in Ukrainian.
