# Product Pivot: B2C Digital Pet Passport

## Decision

VetCare MVP now starts as a B2C utility for pet owners. The primary promise is a digital pet passport in the owner's phone: pet profile, medical notes, preventive care, documents, reminders, PDF summary, and a safe QR public mini-card.

## Why

The clinic marketplace depends on supply, verified schedules, operational support, and trust from clinics. A pet-owner passport can be tested earlier, creates repeat utility, and still prepares structured data for later clinic workflows.

## Product Center

The pet medical card is the product center. Every feature must make the pet passport more complete, easier to keep current, or safer to share.

## Kept

- Pet profiles and medical cards.
- Visit records and clinic documents as historical/pilot data.
- Clinic cabinet for B2B pilots.
- Supabase RLS as the source of truth for sensitive data.

## Hidden Or Deprioritized

- Clinic marketplace and clinic search as a primary mobile tab.
- Appointment booking as a primary mobile flow.
- Reviews, subscriptions, and notification automation as core MVP value.
- Payments, chat, AI, insurance, telemedicine, and complex CRM.

## New Core

- Digital pet passport.
- Preventive care and vaccination tracking.
- Owner-managed documents.
- In-app reminders.
- QR public mini-card with safe fields only.
- PDF passport summary placeholder.
- Public landing page and waitlist for pet owners.
