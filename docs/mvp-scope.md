# MVP Scope

## In Scope

- Owner account and pet profile.
- Pet fields: photo, species, breed, birth date, sex, weight, color, microchip, sterilization, allergies, chronic conditions, owner contact, emergency contact, notes.
- Health events: vaccination, tick treatment, parasite treatment, deworming, medication, grooming, vet visit, other.
- Documents: vet passport scans, lab results, prescriptions, certificates, vaccination proof, photos, other.
- Reminders: upcoming care dates with manual completion.
- QR and PDF entry point.
- Public waitlist landing page.
- Clinic cabinet kept for pilot workflows.

## Out Of Scope For This Stage

- Payments.
- Chat.
- Push notifications.
- AI features.
- Ratings marketplace.
- Complex CRM.
- Insurance.
- Telemedicine.
- Automated email/SMS reminders.

## Success Shape

A pet owner can create a pet passport, add health context, see reminders and documents, and understand how QR/PDF sharing will work before full export/upload automation is implemented.
