# Beta Release Notes

## Product Summary

VetCare beta helps veterinary clinics manage profiles, services, doctors, schedules, appointments, visit records, private document metadata, reviews, subscriptions, and in-app notifications. Pet owners can manage pets, book appointments, view treatment history, receive updates, and leave reviews.

## Included In Beta

- Flutter pet owner app with mock and Supabase modes.
- Next.js clinic cabinet.
- Supabase Auth, PostgreSQL, Storage-ready metadata, and RLS.
- Pet profiles and medical card foundation.
- Clinic/doctor discovery.
- Appointment booking and clinic appointment management.
- Visit records and private document metadata.
- Reviews and ratings.
- Manual subscription plans and limits.
- In-app notifications and reminder architecture.
- Beta feedback form.
- Privacy, terms, data processing, and support placeholders.

## Not Included Yet

- Real payments.
- Real SMS/email/push providers.
- AI diagnosis.
- Chat.
- Advanced analytics.
- Marketplace ranking.
- Production legal review.
- Automated account deletion/export.

## How Clinics Should Test

1. Complete clinic profile.
2. Add services, doctors, and schedules.
3. Confirm new appointments.
4. Create visit records.
5. Upload visible and hidden documents.
6. Review subscription limits and notifications.
7. Submit beta feedback.

## How Pet Owners Should Test

1. Add pets.
2. Browse clinics and doctors.
3. Book appointment.
4. Review appointment and notifications.
5. Open published visit records and documents.
6. Leave review.
7. Submit beta feedback.

## Privacy Reminder

Use fake/demo data during beta unless a clinic has explicitly agreed to controlled testing. Do not upload real sensitive files in local demos.
